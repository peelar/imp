When your imp needs a key or a login, it fills in the whole form itself (hosts, header and variable for another API, or the site and username for a login) and asks in the chat: you paste the value into the card above the message box and press Save. The value goes straight to your vault, and your imp never sees it. A key's hosts can now be written as addresses, such as https://www.example.com.

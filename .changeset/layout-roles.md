Each part of the window now has one job. The sidebar holds Imp's own pages (Home, Routines, Tricks, the app directory and Settings) and your recent chats. The bar along the bottom holds only your apps, and an app is highlighted only while it is open, with ⌘1 to ⌘9 in the bar's order. The chat stays beside every page except its own. Routines, Tricks and the app directory have their own addresses, so Back works, and **Ask for a routine** types straight into the chat beside the page.

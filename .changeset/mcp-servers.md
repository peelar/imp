Your imp can now use your MCP servers, such as Linear's, Notion's or Sentry's. Add one in Settings → Connections by its address, then sign in to it or paste its key: the sign-in stays in Imp's vault. Your imp finds a server's tools the same way it finds your apps' actions, reads what a server answers as data rather than instructions, and asks you before it changes anything there.

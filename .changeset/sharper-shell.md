On a wide window Imp has a new layout. The sidebar is tinted and lists your apps, so the bar of tabs along the bottom is gone. Home fills the window: a larger greeting, the message box right under it, and your apps as a grid that says how each one stands (a preview, a key it waits for, its last sync), and a green, orange or red mark on your imp's computer says whether it is ready. The chat beside an app is tinted too, and the GitHub app's filters and rows are easier to scan.

Drive Imp from the keyboard: ⌘N starts a new chat from any page, ⌘1 to ⌘9 open the chat, your apps and the Computer, ⌘, opens Settings, ⌘. stops the imp, and ⌘S saves an app's preview. In the Mac app they are in the menu bar too, and holding ⌘ shows each control's keys on the screen.

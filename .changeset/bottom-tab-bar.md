On a wide window your apps are back in a bar along the bottom of the page, and the sidebar holds New chat, Routines, Tricks, the app directory, your recent chats and Settings. Routines get a page of their own, with a button to ask for a new one, and the sidebar no longer shows empty lists or a zero count. An app that a feature flag keeps off, such as GitHub, no longer shows a tab, and its address opens the chat instead.
